# PLocked

An AI study desk that notices when you drift off your goal — on your laptop or your phone — and projects a gentle nudge, a session timer, and your progress onto your desk.

**Team:** Junhui Park, Anika Chadha, Shree Reddi

## Folders

- `backend/` — the brain: collects messages, checks if a site fits your goal, decides when to nudge
- `extension/` — Chrome add-on that reports which site is open
- `vision/` — camera code that detects present, away, or phone in hand
- `app/` — web app for setting your goal, plus the projector view

See `CLAUDE.md` for full project details.
